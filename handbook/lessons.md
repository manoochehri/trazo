# Lessons learned

A short retrospective from Trazo's first published release, `v0.1.0`.

- **Keep one source of truth.** A single home for framework rules makes updates easier to review and keeps each install consistent.
- **Plan around a release goal.** Milestones show what belongs in a release and what remains before it ships.
- **Keep each project's own memory.** Plans and decisions should stay with the project, even when its framework is upgraded.
- **Check what people install.** Verify the published version, not just the copy on a developer's machine.
- **Separate building from reviewing.** A fresh reviewer can spot gaps the builder may miss; leave the review where the team can find it.

These are retrospective notes, not a process for automatically collecting lessons. Durable guidance belongs in the project's rules or decision records.
